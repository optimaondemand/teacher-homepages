Paste this into your course's Syllabus page in Canvas, using the HTML editor.

To put it on your Canvas Syllabus page

  1. Click Copy HTML below.
  2. In Canvas, open your course and click Syllabus, then Edit.
  3. Click the </> button at the bottom right of the editor to switch to the HTML editor.
  4. Select everything that is there and paste over it.
  5. Update Syllabus.

Canvas adds its own list of assignments and dates below whatever you paste. That is normal and nothing to fix.

One thing to know about editing it afterwards

If you go back into the Syllabus page and edit it in the normal rich text view rather than the HTML view, Canvas quietly rewrites some of the styling and the page can lose its look. If you need to change something, change it here and paste the whole thing again.

To hand out a Word copy

Click Download Word. It opens in Word and every part of it can be edited there, so if you would rather finish it by hand, that is the file to use. Anything you left blank in the form arrives as a highlighted placeholder.
